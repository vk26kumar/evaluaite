Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

In short: AI-EvaluAIte helps teachers grade handwritten answer sheets and create question papers. Uploaded pages are sent to Google's Gemini AI to be read and marked, then discarded; we keep the resulting text, marks and papers in your account until you delete them. We don't sell your data or show ads.

Who this policy covers

This policy explains how AI-EvaluAIte ("we", "the service") handles information when you use the website at ai-evaluaite-1.onrender.com and its API. The service is intended for teachers and other educators. Students should not create accounts.

Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, and a securely hashed password if you sign up with email. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo address and Google account ID. We never see your Google password.
  • Profile details you choose to add: role, school or institution, and subjects you teach.
  • Content you provide: answer keys (questions, model answers and marks), student names or roll numbers you enter, photos or PDFs of answer sheets, reference material you upload for question papers, and topics for slide decks.
  • Content the service creates: transcriptions of students' answers, marks, feedback and summaries, question papers and answer keys, and any changes you make to them.
  • Activity history: a record of actions in your account, such as creating, copying, regenerating or deleting a paper, grading a sheet or changing a mark, so you can review them on your profile.
  • Technical data: the time of your last sign-in, and basic request logs (address requested, response status and timing) used to keep the service running. IP addresses are used briefly in memory to limit abuse and are not stored in our database.
  • Stored in your browser: your sign-in session, theme preference and an unsaved answer-key draft are kept in your browser's local storage. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

How uploaded answer sheets are handled

When you submit an answer sheet or reference material, the files are held in our server's memory only while they are processed, sent to Google's Gemini API to be read and marked, and then discarded. They are not saved to disk or to our database. What we keep is the text the AI produced (for example, the transcription and the marks), together with each file's name, type and size.

How we use information

  • To provide the service: grading sheets, generating question papers and slides, and showing your results and history.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To diagnose problems and prevent abuse.

We don't sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it for others' marketing.

Service providers we share data with

  • Google (Gemini API): receives uploaded pages, answer keys, question-paper settings and slide topics in order to produce transcriptions, marks, papers and slides. Google processes this content under the Gemini API terms. Depending on the plan the service uses, Google may use submitted content to improve its products; see Google's Gemini API terms.
  • Google (Sign-In): if you choose "Continue with Google".
  • Google Fonts: the website loads fonts from Google, which receives your IP address.
  • MongoDB Atlas: hosts our database.
  • Render: hosts the website and API.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

Students' information

Answer sheets and marks you upload belong to your students. By using the service you confirm that you are allowed to process this information for teaching purposes, for example under your school's policies and any consent they require. You can enter a roll number instead of a name if you prefer not to store students' names.

How long we keep information

We keep your account and its content until you delete them. You can delete any graded sheet or question paper at any time. Deleting your account (Profile → Account → Delete account) immediately removes your account, papers, graded sheets and history from our database. Copies in backups kept by our database provider are removed as those backups expire.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Each account can see only its own papers, sheets and history. No online service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: view everything on your profile and export student marks as CSV.
  • Correction: edit your profile, adjust any mark and edit generated questions.
  • Deletion: delete individual items or your whole account at any time.
  • For anything else, including requests under your local data protection law, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page and let users know in the app.
